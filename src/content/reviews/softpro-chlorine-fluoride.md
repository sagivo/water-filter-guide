The SoftPro Chlorine+ Fluoride SUPER Filter pairs upflow catalytic carbon with a bone char stage — the media combination aimed squarely at fluoridated city water. Per the maker's spec sheet: 10 GPM, 1,000,000-gallon media capacity, targeting fluoride, chloramine, and PFAS, with no independent certifications found and a lifetime warranty at ~$1,267. **The one-line verdict:** the best pick for fluoridated city water — bone char for the fluoride that standard carbon misses, at a mid-range price with minimal upkeep.

## Who the SoftPro Chlorine+ Fluoride is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it if your city fluoridates and you want whole-house fluoride reduction — bone char is the media that actually targets fluoride, and most carbon systems in this guide don't include it. The 10 GPM rating suits 2–4 bath homes.

Skip it if you need certified performance of any kind — no independent certs were found, so everything here is a maker claim. If PFAS is the real concern rather than a bullet point, the [Aquasana HealthShield](/filters/aquasana-healthshield/) is WQA certified to NSF/ANSI 53 for it (see our [PFAS guide](/posts/pfas-forever-chemicals-filter-guide/)). Skip it if your water isn't fluoridated — then the bone char stage is paying for a problem you don't have, and the [SpringWell CF1](/filters/springwell-cf1/) is cheaper to run.

## Filtration performance

Per the maker's spec sheet: upflow catalytic carbon for chlorine, chloramine, tastes, and odors, plus bone char targeting fluoride — and PFAS listed among the maker's targets too. The honest read: bone char for fluoride is a legitimate media choice, but with no independent certifications found, the chloramine and PFAS claims carry only the maker's word. That's a meaningful gap versus component-certified systems like the [SpringWell CF1](/filters/springwell-cf1/) — worth weighing if those contaminants are your actual worry.

## Flow rate and pressure

Rated 10 GPM — solidly mid-range, covering typical 2–4 bath homes. The upflow design is claimed to reduce channeling through the media bed; whether that translates to measurable performance difference isn't independently documented, so treat it as a design note rather than a proven advantage.

## Installation and maintenance

Standard whole-house tank install on the main line — DIY-possible, pro-common. Maintenance is minimal: media rated to 1,000,000 gallons with upkeep estimated around $40 per year. The lifetime warranty is a strong inclusion at this price point.

## Cost of ownership

Upfront ~$1,267 with about $40 per year in upkeep: five-year ownership lands near ~$1,467 before installation. That's competitive with the SpringWell CF1's five-year total while adding the bone char fluoride stage — the value case is straightforward if fluoride matters to you.
