The SpringWell CF4 is the larger sibling of the CF1: the same catalytic coconut-shell carbon + KDF media formula, scaled up to 12 GPM service flow for homes with 4–6 bathrooms. Per the maker's spec sheet, it carries the same 1,000,000-gallon media capacity and the same NSF/ANSI 42 & 53 component certifications. **The one-line verdict:** if the CF1 fits your home but your flow needs are bigger, this is the step-up — but buy it for the flow rate, not extra filtration power.

## Who the SpringWell CF4 is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it if you have 4–6 bathrooms or regularly run multiple showers, laundry, and irrigation simultaneously — that's what the 12 GPM rating is for. It's the same city-water profile as the CF1: chlorine taste and odor reduction with long-lived media.

Skip it if the [CF1](/filters/springwell-cf1/)'s 9 GPM rating already covers your home — upsizing flow capacity you won't use adds cost without improving filtration. Skip it if you need certified PFAS or lead reduction, or if you're on well water with iron/sulfur issues — those are different problems needing different systems, like the [APEC CB3](/filters/apec-cb3/) for iron-heavy wells.

## Filtration performance

The CF4 uses the same media stack as the CF1: catalytic coconut-shell activated carbon plus KDF media, with a 5-micron pre-filter to catch sediment. Per the maker's spec sheet, it targets chlorine, chloramine, and municipal tastes and odors.

Certification status matches the CF1: components certified to NSF/ANSI 42 and 53, not whole-system certified. That's stronger than maker-claims-only, but if you want independently certified PFAS reduction, the [Aquasana HealthShield](/filters/aquasana-healthshield/) (WQA certified to NSF/ANSI 53) is the more defensible pick — see our [PFAS guide](/posts/pfas-forever-chemicals-filter-guide/) for context.

## Flow rate and pressure

Rated at 12 GPM service flow, the CF4 is aimed at 4–6 bath homes where peak demand is the constraint. If you're unsure where your home sits, run through the [flow sizer](/tools/flow-sizer/) — buying 12 GPM when 9 GPM would do is money left on the table.

## Installation and maintenance

Installation mirrors the CF1: a single tank with a pre-filter housing on your main water line. DIY is possible for comfortable owners, but cutting the main line is plumbing — many people hire it out.

Maintenance is minimal. The tank media is rated for up to 1,000,000 gallons, and upkeep is estimated around $35 per year for the sediment pre-filter. SpringWell offers a lifetime warranty on the tank and valves plus a 6-month satisfaction guarantee.
