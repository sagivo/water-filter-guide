The Express Water Heavy Metal 3-Stage is a budget Big Blue system with a few thoughtful extras: per the maker's spec sheet, KDF + polyphosphate anti-scale + carbon stages target heavy metals, chlorine, and scale, and the unit ships with a stainless stand and pressure gauges — uncommon at ~$548–$550. Flow is rated 15 GPM (the maker also lists 0.25 gal/sec), capacity 100,000 gallons, and the maker says the filters are NSF certified. **The one-line verdict:** the budget heavy-metal pick — a well-kitted Big Blue system for buyers who want anti-scale and gauges included, priced like the budget class it belongs to.

## Who the Express Water Heavy Metal is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it if heavy metals are a specific concern on a budget, and you value the practical extras — gauges that tell you when cartridges are spent, and a stand that makes mounting easy. The anti-scale stage also suits moderately hard water.

Skip it if you need verified, certified heavy-metal reduction — the NSF claim is the maker's, unverified by us. The [SpringWell LCR](/filters/springwell-lcr/) is the lead-focused alternative with NSF/ANSI 53 components. Skip it if your water is iron-heavy well water — the [APEC CB3](/filters/apec-cb3/) is built around KDF85 for exactly that.

## Filtration performance

Three stages per the maker's spec sheet: KDF media, polyphosphate anti-scale, and carbon. The stated targets are heavy metals, chlorine, taste/odor, and scale. On the NSF claim: the maker says the filters are NSF certified, but we couldn't verify the specific certification marks — so file this as a maker claim until confirmed, a notch below even component-level certifications like those on the [SpringWell CF1](/filters/springwell-cf1/).

## Flow rate and pressure

Rated 15 GPM (0.25 gallons per second, per the maker). The pressure gauges are genuinely useful here: watch for the pressure differential climbing across stages, and you'll know the cartridges are loading up before flow visibly suffers.

## Installation and maintenance

The stainless stand simplifies mounting — freestanding in a utility room rather than lag-bolted to a wall. Otherwise it's standard Big Blue DIY plumbing. Maintenance is annual-ish: ~$140 per year in replacement media per the maker's figures, with the gauges guiding timing. The 1-year warranty matches the budget class.

## Cost of ownership

Upfront ~$548–$550 plus about $140 per year: five-year ownership lands near ~$1,248–$1,250 before installation. That's the most expensive of the budget trio (iSpring WGB32B, this, the KDS) — the gauges, stand, and anti-scale stage account for the premium. ## Ownership math and close alternatives

The Express Water Heavy Metal 3-Stage typically sells for ~$548–$550. With ~$160/year upkeep, five-year ownership lands near ~$1,348 plus installation. The media is rated for cartridges roughly every 6–12 months depending on water quality, and it's backed by a 1-year limited warranty.

The 15 GPM rating covers larger homes and 4+ bathroom households with heavy simultaneous use. Whole-house pressure also depends on your plumbing and incoming pressure, so run your numbers through our [flow sizer](/tools/flow-sizer/) before committing.
